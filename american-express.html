The challenge

Fragmented product and engineering workflows slowed delivery. The opportunity was to build shared capabilities and a delivery model that could work across business units and teams.

A platform teams could adopt

Led development and global rollout of an internal platform. Drove adoption across 5 organizations and 12 teams through reusable capabilities, standardized delivery processes, and workflow automation.

The result

Reduced launch timelines from approximately 9 months to 2–3 weeks and improved operations team efficiency by approximately 90%. Supported capabilities serving more than 50M customers worldwide. Helped launch an in-house A/B and multivariate testing platform that saved approximately $5M annually.
